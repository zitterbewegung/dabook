# Instructions for research agents

- Do not solve a problem by changing its hypotheses or its counting convention.
- Always distinguish point sets from ordered parameterizations; distinguish
  finite evidence from a uniform theorem.
- Every theorem needs exact quantified hypotheses, proof, source, dependencies,
  and a claim status. `REPORTED_UNVERIFIED` is the default for inaccessible
  previous-chat proof claims.
- Run prior-art searches for new novelty assertions, especially for finite-field
  incidence identities and claimed solutions of long-standing problems.
- Do not manufacture Lean files, citations, numerical runs, or proof certificates.
- Keep failed approaches, original source material, and correction history.
- When revising, maintain all unrelated code and artifacts; include complete
  replacements and tests.
